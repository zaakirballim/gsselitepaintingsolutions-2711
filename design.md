# GSS Elite — design direction
Cinematic full-screen 3D house story inspired by the supplied video. Dark charcoal, amber atmospheric light, editorial Cormorant Garamond headings with Poppins body. Fresh terracotta, cream, teal and sunshine wall finishes.
Fixed slender navigation; sticky four-stage scroll story. Real WebGL modern two-storey house, glazing, balcony, roof slabs, stairs, trees and planters. Paint washes upward as the story advances, camera gently rotates. Colour selector lets visitors preview finishes. Left-aligned text, right progress rail, bottom scroll cue. Editorial services rows then contact form.
Tokens: background #101410, text #f5f1e8, muted #a9aca2, accent #dcf48a, copper #ca7857. 8px spacing, pill buttons, max width 1440px.
Accessibility: semantic labels, keyboard controls, mobile layout, reduced-motion disables camera travel. Form opens user's email app addressed to the business, never claims server delivery.
Web only. No backend needed for enquiry email composition. Address: 1571 Raven Street, Johannesburg, Gauteng.

Realistic material roughness and studio environment reflections. Automatic scroll palette: bare plaster → sage → coastal blue → terracotta → warm ivory. Manual swatches remain previews, released by further scrolling. Preserve camera story and mobile readability.
